Welcome to the WI Public Transit Reachability Explorer!

Search for an address or click the map, choose what mode you'll use to reach the transit stops, and your desired travel time, then generate a reachability area. Use the filters to find stops with specific accessibility features.

Disclaimer: Transit stop data is aggregated from OpenStreetMap, accurate as of October 1, 2026. Transit stop data may vary in quality and completeness. Any missing data will be marked as 'not specified' or similar.

About Filters

  • Wheelchair-accessible: Visible stops are wheelchair-accessible.
  • Has shelter: Visible stops have some kind of shelter.
  • Has seating: Visible stops have a bench or other seating.
  • Has tactile paving: Visible stops have tactile paving, or textured ground surfaces by curbs or stairs to assist the visually impaired.
  • Is well-lit: Visible stops have sufficient lighting nearby.

Tip: Multiple filters may be selected at once, if you require more than one accessibility feature in your desired public transport stops.

About Modes

The exact speeds for which travel times are calculated are a bit convoluted in some cases. Note that these travel times are averages.

  • Walk: 5 km/h, unless a path has an internal 'trail difficulty' value greater than 1, then 2 km/h.
  • Cycling: Depends on internal defaults based on surface, highway type, and track type. Read the documentation below for more details.
  • Drive: Depends on a cascading priority system. First, depends on speed limit. Second, average speed based on country. Third, the type of way (highway, etc.). Fourth, unpaved track type. This value is then adjusted based on surface type, residential area status, and if there is a roundabout present. Read the documentation below for more details.
  • Wheelchair: 4 km/h, modified based on parameters, such as the presence of sidewalks and type of path. In practice, wheelchair speed ranges between 3 and 10 km/h.

If you are interested in reading more on this, the documentation is provided here.
